Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 14, 2026

Hexbound ("we", "our", "us") is a mobile game developed by Artem Osetrov. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect when you use the Hexbound app ("the App"), how we use it, and the choices you have.

1. Information We Collect

Everything below is linked to your account and is used only to run the game. None of it is used to track you.

  • Account Information: your email address and username when you register. If you choose Sign in with Apple or Google Sign-In, we receive the account identifier and email address that service shares with us (with Sign in with Apple this can be a private relay address). Passwords are handled by our authentication provider and stored only as a hash.
  • Guest Play Identifier: if you play as a guest, the App creates a random identifier and keeps it in your device's Keychain so you can return to the same character. It is not an advertising identifier and is not shared with anyone.
  • Game Data: characters, progress, inventory, combat history, achievements, ratings and in-game currency balances.
  • Player Messages and Reports: character names, messages you send to other players, and reports and blocks you make, so we can deliver messages and moderate abuse.
  • Purchase Records: which in-app product you bought, the App Store transaction identifier and its date, so we can deliver what you paid for and prevent fraud. Payment is handled entirely by Apple; we never receive your card or bank details.
  • Product Interaction: in-game events such as screens opened and features used, linked to your user ID, so we can fix problems and balance the game.
  • Push Notification Token: if you allow notifications, the token Apple issues for your device, so we can send game notifications. You can turn notifications off at any time in iOS Settings.
  • Device Information: device model, operating system version and app version, for compatibility and troubleshooting.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Provide, maintain and improve the game.
  • Authenticate your account and keep your game data secure.
  • Verify in-app purchases and deliver gems, items and subscription benefits.
  • Run matchmaking, ratings and leaderboards.
  • Deliver player messages and handle reports, blocks and moderation.
  • Send notifications you have allowed, and important account messages.
  • Analyze how features are used to fix bugs and balance the game.

3. No Tracking, No Ads

Hexbound does not track you. We do not link your data with data from other companies' apps or websites for advertising or measurement, we do not use the advertising identifier (IDFA), we do not show third-party ads, and we do not sell, rent or share your personal information with data brokers or advertisers.

4. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored with Supabase (hosted on AWS infrastructure) with encryption in transit and at rest. We use rate limiting, server-side validation and secure authentication to protect it.

5. Service Providers

We use these providers to run the game. They process data only on our behalf:

  • Supabase: authentication and database hosting.
  • Vercel: backend API hosting.
  • Upstash: rate limiting and short-lived server cache.
  • Apple: in-app purchases, Sign in with Apple, and push notification delivery (APNs).
  • Google: Google Sign-In, only if you choose to sign in with Google.

6. Age Requirement

Hexbound is intended for adults. It is rated 18+ on the App Store because it contains simulated gambling played with in-game currency only (Shell Game, Fortune Wheel and Fortune's Table). We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18. If you believe a minor has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

7. Your Rights

You can:

  • Access your game data in your in-game profile, or ask us for a copy at the address below.
  • Update your account information in the App's settings.
  • Delete your account and all associated data in Settings → Account → Delete Account.

Account deletion is permanent. All characters, progress, items and currency are removed. Purchase history held by Apple is governed by Apple's own privacy policy.

8. Data Retention

We keep your data while your account is active. When you delete your account, your personal and game data are deleted from our servers.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will tell you about significant changes in the App. The "Effective Date" at the top shows when it was last revised.
